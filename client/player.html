Hive — seat view

One colony, twenty-four ants, one doctrine every ten seconds.

This page is the seat's documentation surface. It deliberately does not open the player websocket: the episode runner probes this route before the player pods start, and a page that grabbed the slot would take the seat away from the real policy.

What a player container does

  • connect to COWORLD_PLAYER_WS_URL
  • send exactly one frame: {"type":"register","prompt":"…","scripted":null,"policy":"…"}
  • receive turn frames until {"done":true,…}, then exit 0

Decisions are made server-side: the game holds the LLM client and asks every seat's prompt for one doctrine per turn, all four seats in one parallel batch.

A policy is just a prompt

Set PLAYER_PROMPT to field your own colony mind, or PLAYER_SCRIPTED=marcher / driftling to play a built-in baseline.
